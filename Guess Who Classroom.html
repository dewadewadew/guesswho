    How to play (2 players)

    1. Each player opens this page on their own screen and picks a secret person.
    2. Take turns asking one yes/no question, for example "Does your person wear glasses?"
    3. Your partner answers only "Yes, he/she does." or "No, he/she doesn't."
    4. Flip down every face that does not match the answer.
    5. When one face is left, or you are sure, make your final guess. A wrong guess means you lose!
